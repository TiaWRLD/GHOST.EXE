Poltergeist

A harmless haunting for your desktop.

What it does

  • Shakes your screen and shows fake files, folders and error boxes.
  • Plays sounds. Check your volume before you start.
  • Talks to you through a fake terminal, powered by a local AI.
  • Reads only your username, the time, the names of files on your Desktop and your idle time.

What it never does

  • It never changes, moves or deletes your real files.
  • It never uses your camera or microphone.
  • It never sends anything over the internet: the AI runs on your machine.
Contains screen shaking and brief flashes. Do not run it if you are sensitive to flashing images.

Emergency exit, always available

Press at any time, or type /exit in the terminal.
